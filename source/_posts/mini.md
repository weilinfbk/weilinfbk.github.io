🐱 Basic Drop Symbols
 * Numbers (1, 2, 3, 4): Sequential Drops. Cats drop in sequence, looping infinitely from Lane 1 → Lane 2 → Lane 3 → Lane 4.
 * Exclamation Mark (!): Pure Random. Cats jump completely randomly across all 4 lanes with no pattern at all.
 * At Symbol (@): Non-Consecutive Random. Also random, but a cat will never fall in the exact same lane twice in a row (prevents back-to-back hits on the same lane).
 * Hash/Pound (#): Jack/Stream. Always drops repeatedly in the same lane. If you only enter #, the cat will stubbornly stick to just one lane.
 * Ampersand (&): Mirror Symmetry. If the previous cat was in Lane 1, the next cat is guaranteed to be in the last lane (Lane 4).
 * D (Double): 2 cats drop simultaneously, requiring two fingers to tap at once.
 * T (Triple): 3 cats drop simultaneously, requiring three fingers to tap at once.
 * A (All): 4 cats drop simultaneously—cats across the entire screen, yay!!




🐱 Combo Mechanics
You can chain these symbols together! Combining them creates the "one-tap presets" you see on the homepage:
 * Trill (Enter 32): Cats alternate back and forth between Lane 3 and Lane 2 (3-2-3-2-3-2).
 * Minijack (Enter @#): Drops a random cat, followed immediately by another cat in the exact same lane.
 * Stairs/Staircase (Enter 1234321): Drops from Lane 1 to 4, then climbs back down from 4 to 1.

🐱 Advanced / Beginner-Friendly Recommendations (Rarely Used)
 * +1 and -1 (Shift): Shift position. For example, if the previous cat was in Lane 2, +1 moves it to Lane 3, while -1 moves it to Lane 1.
 * *2: Drops randomly within a specific set of lanes.
 * Example setup: @#D+1%13
   · @: First, drop a non-repeating random cat.
   · #: Drop another cat in the exact same lane (Minijack).
   · D: Drop a Double (2 cats at once).
   · +1: Shift one lane to the right based on the previous Double position.
   · %1 3: Finally, drop a random cat between Lanes 1 and 3.




💡 Fun "Recipes" to Try
You can copy and paste the string combinations below directly into the "Pattern / Key Layout" box, then tap Done to test them out:
 * 1#2#3#4#
   Effect: Double tap on Lane 1, double tap on Lane 2, double tap on Lane 3... very rhythmic!
 * !@
   Effect: Drops a completely random cat, immediately followed by a non-repeating random cat.
 * 1234#
   Effect: Runs a staircase pattern, then finishes with a double tap on the last key.

How to Play:
Once you've entered your string, tap Done, then tap Start. Watch closely how the cats fall at the bottom of the screen, and you'll get the hang of it instantly! If a wrong input causes the screen to freeze, simply tap the "Bug Refresh" button on the homepage to clear it.
